Navigate the RTL schematic
Drag the canvas to pan, use the mouse wheel or toolbar for zoom, and double-click a module to enter. Click any routed wire to inspect its signal; hold Ctrl/Cmd while selecting to track through proven direct port bindings.
Contextual actions: right-click a generated group to expand only that group, or right-click an individual member to collapse it. Module actions show contextual SystemVerilog and lint. These are intentionally absent from the global toolbar.
Keyboard: / focuses symbol search; Esc closes menus or clears selection; + and − zoom; the accessible element list works without a mouse.
